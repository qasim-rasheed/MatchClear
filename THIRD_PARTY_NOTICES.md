## Tritan simulation

The tritan two-plane parameters and projection approach are adapted from libDaltonLens by Nicolas Burrus, released into the public domain under the Unlicense:
https://github.com/DaltonLens/libDaltonLens/blob/master/libDaltonLens.c

The reference uses Brettel, Viénot and Mollon (1997), with sRGB/Smith–Pokorny conversion and RGB white as the neutral point. The partial tritan view is a 0.5 interpolation in linear RGB, an approximation rather than a clinically validated severity model.
